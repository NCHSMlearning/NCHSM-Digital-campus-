How to Apply to NCHSM: A Complete Step-by-Step Guide 2026

August 30, 2026 6 min read NCHSM Admin

Are you ready to start your journey in healthcare education? Applying to Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management (NCHSM) is straightforward. This step-by-step guide will walk you through the entire process.

1. Choose Your Program

NCHSM offers a wide range of programs in health sciences and management. The first step is to choose the program that matches your career goals.

  • KRCHN - Kenya Registered Community Health Nursing — A 3-year program preparing students for nursing practice.
  • TVET Diploma Programs — Including Perioperative Theatre Technology, Community Health, Health Records and IT, Social Work, ICT, Medical Engineering, and more.
  • TVET Certificate Programs — Including Patient Care, Caregiver, Orthopedic Trauma Medicine, Agriculture, and more.
  • Artisan Programs — Including Community Health, Agriculture, and Social Work.

Visit our programs page to explore all options.

2. Check Admission Requirements

Each program has specific requirements. In general, you'll need:

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) with minimum grades
  • Relevant subjects depending on your chosen program
  • A valid National ID or Passport
  • Passport-size photographs

3. Collect Required Documents

Before starting your application, gather all necessary documents:

  • KCSE certificate or result slip
  • Birth certificate or National ID
  • Passport-size photos
  • Medical examination form (for nursing programs)
  • Recommendation letters (if required)

4. Complete the Application Form

Applications can be submitted online or in person at the NCHSM campus. The application form requires personal details, educational background, and program choice.

Apply online by visiting nchsm.co.ke or visit the admission office at our Kiamunyi campus.

5. Submit Your Application

After filling out the application form, submit it along with all required documents. Online applications can be submitted through our portal. In-person applications can be dropped at the admission office.

Payment of the application fee is also required at this stage.

6. Await Admission Decision

After submission, NCHSM reviews applications regularly. You can expect to receive feedback within 2-3 weeks. Successful applicants will be offered admission and provided with joining instructions.

7. Accept Your Offer and Enroll

Upon receiving an admission offer, confirm your acceptance and complete the enrollment process. This includes paying the first installment of fees and registering for classes.

Conclusion

Applying to NCHSM is a straightforward process. Follow this step-by-step guide, and you'll be on your way to a rewarding career in healthcare. Ready to apply? Start your application today →
