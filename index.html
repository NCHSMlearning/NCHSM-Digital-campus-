Welcome to NCHSM

Learning for healthcare, community service and professional growth.

Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management is dedicated to providing education and practical training in health sciences and related professional fields.

The college's learning environment brings together classroom instruction, practical sessions, skills development, academic guidance and other activities that help students connect theory with real-world practice.

NCHSM's public information helps prospective students understand available programmes, admission processes, learning opportunities and digital student services.

Beyond the Classroom

Practical Training & Skills Development

Health and technical professionals need opportunities to connect theory with practice. NCHSM's learning environment supports classroom instruction alongside practical and skills development activities.

01 • SKILLS

Skills Development

Guided practical activities can help learners develop technical competence, communication and safe working practices appropriate to their programme.

02 • APPLICATION

Applying Knowledge

Students are encouraged to connect concepts learned in class with practical situations relevant to their field of training.

03 • PROFESSIONALISM

Professional Development

Learning also develops teamwork, communication, responsibility, ethics and service-oriented professional behaviour.

Clinical and practical placement information: Clinical placement sites, schedules, requirements and approved learning activities should be published according to the current requirements of each programme and relevant authority.
