Leadership & direction

A Message from the Director

Ms. Suzan Nyamai Director, Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management

At Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management, we are committed to nurturing competent, compassionate, ethical and skilled professionals who can make a meaningful difference in the lives of individuals, families and communities.

Our approach to education combines academic knowledge with practical learning, professional discipline and a commitment to quality. We encourage our students to embrace lifelong learning, uphold integrity and develop the confidence and skills needed to serve in a changing professional environment.

As you begin or continue your educational journey with us, we welcome you to a learning community that values excellence, responsibility, teamwork and service. Together, we can contribute to a healthier future for our communities.

Learn, Practise, Serve and Excel.

Proposed message for the Director’s review and approval.
