Resource Centre

Information for students, applicants and the community

The NCHSM Downloads Centre provides a central place for visitors to access publicly available college information, educational resources, notices and selected institutional documents.

Prospective students can use these resources to learn more about the college, programmes, admissions and academic life before beginning their application process.

Important Notice

Only documents intended for public access should be published here. Private academic records, examination results, financial information and other restricted student information remain accessible through the appropriate secure NCHSM systems.
