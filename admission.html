About NCHSM

Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management (NCHSM) is a premier institution offering quality health sciences education. We are accredited by TVETA and offer a wide range of Diploma, Certificate, and Artisan programs in Nursing, Healthcare, ICT, and Applied Sciences.

Nursing Council of Kenya TVETCDACC

Application Process

  1. Click on "Apply Now" to create your account.
  2. Login with your new credentials.
  3. Fill in the required information in each step of the application.
  4. Upload your KCSE certificate, ID, and recommendation letter.
  5. Submit your application for review.
  6. Once approved, you will be notified via email.
