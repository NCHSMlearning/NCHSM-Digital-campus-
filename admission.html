Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using the NCHSM online admissions portal.

How do I start an application?

Click Apply Now, create your applicant account, then sign in and complete the application form.

Can I save my application and continue later?

Yes. Use Save Draft or Save Progress. Your application can be continued after you sign in again.

What documents should I prepare?

Prepare the documents requested in the application, including identification, academic documents and a passport photo where applicable.

Can I change information before submitting?

Yes. Review and update your information in the application steps before final submission.

What if I need help?

Use the Contact page to send an enquiry to the admissions team.
