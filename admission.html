Application Process

To join NCHSM, follow the steps below:

  1. Decide the program you want to undertake and ensure you meet the qualifications.
  2. Click on "Create Account" at the top or "Apply Now" below.
  3. Fill in the required information in each step of the application.
  4. Finish the application process and submit your application.
  5. Once submitted successfully, you will receive an email with your Application ID.
  6. Pay the indicated application fee (see payment details below).
  7. Track your application status by logging in with your Application ID and Password.

Requirements & Payment Details

Documents Required
  • Passport Photo – Clear passport-sized photo (JPG, PNG)
  • National ID / Birth Certificate – Clear copy (both sides for ID)
  • KCSE Certificate / Results Slip – Final high school results
  • Recommendation Letter – From church or school
  • Academic Transcript – For transfer students only
