Programmes at NCHSM

Explore the academic and professional training programmes currently listed by Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management. Use the search tool to find a programme quickly, then review the admissions guidance before applying.

Choose your area of study

NCHSM offers training across nursing, community health, health information, healthcare technology, laboratory sciences, ICT and related professional areas.

Nursing & Patient Care

Training areas include Community Health Nursing, Kenya Registered Nursing, Nursing Assistant and Caregiver programmes.

Community & Social Services

Programmes cover community health, social work, community development and home-based care.

Health Technology & Information

Explore health records and IT, biomedical engineering, science laboratory and perioperative theatre technology.

Important: Entry requirements, duration, programme availability and intake information can change. Confirm current details with NCHSM before making an application.
