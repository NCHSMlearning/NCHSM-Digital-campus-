About Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management

Excellence in Healthcare Education Since 1978

Our Campus

Located in the serene suburb of Kiamunyi, along the Nakuru–Kabarak Road off Canon Ndungu Street, Nakuru College of Health Sciences and Management offers a peaceful and conducive learning environment just 5 kilometres from Nakuru Central Business District.

Our modern campus is thoughtfully designed to inspire learning, encourage collaboration, and provide students with an exceptional educational experience. Spacious, well-ventilated lecture halls are equipped to support interactive and engaging teaching, creating a comfortable environment where students can thrive academically.

Our Facilities

Beyond the classroom, the college boasts modern facilities that enable students to gain practical experience and strengthen their technical competencies:

Science Laboratory

Our well-equipped Science Laboratory supports quality learning through practical experiments and scientific investigation. Students gain valuable experience by applying classroom concepts in a controlled environment.

Skills Laboratory

Our modern Skills Laboratory provides students with a safe, hands-on learning environment equipped with industry-standard training equipment and simulation models to develop essential clinical skills.

Computer Laboratory (ICT Hub)

Featuring modern computers, reliable internet access, and relevant software, the ICT Hub supports research, e-learning, assignments, digital literacy, and essential workplace technology skills.

Library

Stocked with a wide collection of textbooks, journals, reference materials, and digital resources, our Library provides a quiet, conducive environment for study, research, and personal development.

Sports

We believe education extends beyond the classroom. Our Sports Department promotes physical fitness, teamwork, discipline, and leadership through a variety of indoor and outdoor sporting activities.

Student Support

We provide comprehensive support services including academic advising, career counseling, mentorship, and guidance to help students succeed throughout their academic journey.

Our Vision

👁️ Vision

To produce an excellent professional health care provider of international standards

🎯 Mission

To train a committed, highly qualified and self-driven service provider

Why Choose NCHSM

  • Accredited Programs — All programs are accredited by the Nursing Council of Kenya, TVET Authority, and KNQA.
  • Experienced Faculty — Our lecturers are experienced healthcare professionals with years of practice and teaching experience.
  • Modern Facilities — State-of-the-art laboratories, computer hubs, and library resources.
  • Clinical Placements — Students gain hands-on experience through clinical placements in hospitals and health centers.
  • Conducive Environment — Peaceful campus just 5 km from Nakuru CBD.
